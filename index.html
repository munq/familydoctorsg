Thank you for 35 years

After more than three and a half decades on Upper Thomson Road, The Family Doctor clinic has closed for good. A message from Dr Daniel Chia and the nurses.

A deep-felt thank you

Well, after 35 and a half years, we are sad to announce that our clinic has finally closed for good.

We’ve had an incredible run… many of our patients have grown up with us and we were already seeing the third and fourth generations in families that have continued to trust us all these years.

Words cannot ever adequately express how much of an honour and privilege it has been for me and my faithful nurses, Christine, Helen and Jeannie, to have had your trust in us for all these almost 36 years, and I am personally deeply grateful to you all, our beloved patients and now friends, for all these incredible years of friendship and support that you have given me.

You all know how to reach me. I may not be able to be your family doctor any more, but I would love for our friendship to continue. Please keep in touch with me and let me know how you and your family do in the years to come. I will miss you all deeply.

With all our heart,Dr Daniel ChiaChristine, Helen and Jeannie

6th November 2026
