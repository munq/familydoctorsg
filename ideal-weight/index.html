Ideal weight

20 April 2017

Is my weight ideal?

There are two ways to check whether your weight is in a healthy range.

1. Body Mass Index (BMI)

BMI is an index of obesity in adults, based on your height and weight. It is calculated as:

BMI = weight (kg) ÷ [height (m) × height (m)]

BMI for adults and risk of heart disease and diabetes
BMI (kg/m²)Risk category
Below 18.5Risk of nutritional deficiency diseases and osteoporosis
18.5 – 22.9Low risk (healthy range)
23.0 – 27.4Moderate risk
27.5 and aboveHigh risk

2. Desirable body weight range

You can also compare your weight against the desirable range for your height and body frame (small, medium or large). Weights are in kilograms in light clothing, with height in centimetres.
