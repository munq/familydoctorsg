Privacy Policy

Our commitment

The Family Doctor values the privacy of everyone who visits this website. This policy explains what personal information the site receives and collects, and how it is safeguarded. We never sell your personal information to third parties.

Log files

Like most websites, this site’s hosting provider records standard server log data, such as your IP address, internet service provider, browser type, the time of your visit and the pages you view.

Cookies and web beacons

Cookies may be used to remember your preferences, for example so that a message is only shown once per visit.

Advertising cookies

Google may use DoubleClick DART cookies to serve ads based on browsing history. These cookies use non-personally identifiable information and do not record your name, contact details or financial account numbers. You can opt out of personalised advertising through Google Ads Settings.

Managing cookies

You can disable cookies entirely, or block them selectively, through your browser settings or security software. Doing so may affect how some websites work. Your browser vendor’s website has detailed instructions.
